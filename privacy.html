About mail-service

mail-service is an internal application used to integrate with Gmail through the Google Gmail API.

The application is intended for authorised users within the organisation and is not intended for general public use.

Google Gmail API

mail-service requests access to authorised Gmail account data only when required to provide its intended functionality.

Access to Gmail data is controlled through Google's OAuth authorisation process and is limited to authorised users.

Privacy

For information about how mail-service handles information accessed through the Gmail API, please see our Privacy Policy.

Contact

For questions about mail-service or its use of Gmail data, please contact the application administrator within your organisation.
